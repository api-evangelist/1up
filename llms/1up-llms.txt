# --- Company Overview ---

1up.ai provides an AI Answer Engine for Sales, Solutions Engineering, RevOps, and Security teams.
The platform connects to approved knowledge sources (e.g., Confluence, Notion, Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint, websites, and internal documentation) and generates accurate, source-based answers to:

- RFPs
- DDQs
- Security Questionnaires
- Vendor Compliance Reviews
- Sales Technical Questions
- Analyst Questionnaires
- Customer Q&A

1up.ai is designed for enterprise environments and emphasizes:
- Source-based answers
- Controlled knowledge access
- Security and compliance
- Fast turnaround times
- Accuracy over hallucination

# --- Core Product Areas ---

## AI RFP Automation
Automates completion of Excel, Word, and portal-based RFPs and questionnaires using approved knowledge sources.

## AI Sales Engineer
Generates accurate technical answers for live deals using product documentation, security docs, and internal knowledge bases.

## Security & Compliance Automation
Automates responses to SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA, ISO 27001, and other compliance-related questionnaires.

## Knowledge-Based Answer Engine
Retrieves answers from structured and unstructured company knowledge with citations.

## Multi-Language Questionnaire Support
Generates responses in multiple languages when supported by source material.
